# Google Flow videos: each scene in one video, with its lines

Each scene is **one video**: **at home** (Dad and Aniket), **the bookstore**, **at the counter** (the shopkeeper and
Aniket), **the shoe shop**, **back home**, and **the party at home**. Every line is the game's own words, in the
game's order, with the second it starts.

## How to make a scene's video

1. In Google Flow, start **Frames to Video** with Veo, the newest version that makes sound. Choose **landscape
   (16:9)**, **1080p** and **10 seconds**.
2. **First frame:** upload the picture named under the scene, from this game's folder. Paste the **Start** prompt
   (the grey box).
3. A scene with more to say has **Extend** prompts. In Flow's scene builder, add the clip, choose **Extend**, paste
   the next prompt, and repeat. Flow carries the same people and place on from where the clip ended, so it stays one
   video.
4. Where a scene moves to another place (the bookstore: outside, then inside; the shoe shop: the stand, then the
   counter), it has a **second shot**. Make it the same way from its own picture, and add it to the same scene in
   Flow, or send it to me separately and I'll join it.
5. Check the video. The camera stays still. The lips move only on the lines, in time with the words. Nobody's face,
   hair or clothes change. No words or subtitles appear on screen. If anything is wrong, make that clip again.
6. Download each scene as one MP4, named as it says (for example `flow-1-home.mp4`), put them in one folder, and tell
   me where.

**The times:** they come from the game's voices, so the game's voices fit the mouths. If Flow makes a clip shorter
than 10 seconds and the last line doesn't fit, move that line into the next Extend.

**The pictures are WebP** (in `assets/story/` and `assets/party/`). If Flow won't take a WebP, open it in Preview and
choose File → Export → PNG, or ask me for PNG copies.

**Sound:** the game plays its own voices over the video: Swifty's recording, and the boy's, the father's and the
shopkeeper's voices. The video's own sound is turned off, unless you like Flow's voices better (tell me). Swifty isn't
in these videos, because the game draws her; where she explains something between two lines, the game holds the video
while she talks.

## The people (the same all the way)

- **Aniket:** a boy of about twelve, with short, messy dark-brown hair, a green T-shirt, a navy backpack worn on both
  shoulders, blue jeans and white sneakers. His voice is a young Indian boy's, bright and eager.
- **His father:** a light-blue shirt, a moustache and short black hair. His voice is a warm, calm Indian man's.
- **The bookshop's shopkeeper:** glasses, short black hair, a light-blue shirt and a navy apron. His voice is
  friendly and polite.
- **The shoe shop's shopkeeper:** a teal polo shirt and a short beard. He doesn't speak.
