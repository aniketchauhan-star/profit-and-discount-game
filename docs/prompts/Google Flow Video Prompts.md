# Google Flow videos: one video for each scene, with its lines

Six scenes, each one video: **at home**, **the bookstore**, **the counter**, **the shoe shop**, **back home**, and
**the party at home**. A scene with many lines is made in **parts**; each part is one video made in Google Flow, and
I join a scene's parts into one video afterwards. You won't see the joins, because every part starts and ends on the
same picture from the game.

Every line below is the game's own words, in the game's order, with the second it starts in the video.

## How to make one part

1. In Google Flow, start **Frames to Video** with Veo, the newest version that makes sound.
2. Choose **landscape (16:9)**, **1080p**, and **10 seconds**. If Flow only gives 8 seconds, that works too: every
   part's lines end by 7.5 seconds.
3. **First frame:** upload the picture named under the part, from this game's folder. **Last frame:** the same
   picture, unless the part names a different one.
4. Paste the part's prompt, the grey box under it.
5. Check the video. The lips move only on the lines, and nobody's face, hair or clothes change. No words or subtitles
   appear on screen. It ends on the picture with every mouth closed. If anything is wrong, make it again.
6. Download it as MP4 and name it as the part says (for example `flow-1a.mp4`). Put them all in one folder and tell
   me where. I'll join each scene's parts, put them in the game, and time the game's voices to the lips.

**The pictures are WebP** (in `assets/story/` and `assets/party/`). If Flow won't take a WebP, open it in Preview and
choose File → Export → PNG, or ask me for PNG copies.

**Sound:** the game plays its own voices over the video: Swifty's recording, and the boy's, the father's and the
shopkeeper's voices. The video's own sound is turned off. The lines are in the prompts so the mouths move with the
right words at the right time. Swifty isn't in these videos: the game draws her.

## The people (the same in every part)

- **Aniket:** a boy of about twelve, with short, messy dark-brown hair, a green T-shirt, a navy backpack worn on both
  shoulders, blue jeans and white sneakers. His voice is a young Indian boy's, bright and eager.
- **His father:** a light-blue shirt, a moustache and short black hair. His voice is a warm, calm Indian man's.
- **The bookshop's shopkeeper:** glasses, short black hair, a light-blue shirt and a navy apron. His voice is
  friendly and polite.
- **The shoe shop's shopkeeper:** a teal polo shirt and a short beard. He doesn't speak.
