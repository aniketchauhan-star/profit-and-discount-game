# Google Flow videos: each scene in one video, with its lines

Each scene is **one video**: **at home** (Dad and Amit), **the bookstore**, **at the counter** (the shopkeeper and
Amit), **the shoe shop**, **back home**, and **the party at home**. Every line is the game's own words, in the
game's order, with the second it starts.

## How to make a scene's video

1. In Google Flow, choose **Frames to Video**, **landscape (16:9)** and **1080p**. Make each part **the length it
   says** (10, 8 or 6 seconds), so that it ends where its words and movement end, with no still moment left over.
2. **Part 1:** for its first frame, upload the picture named under the scene, from this game's folder. Paste Part 1's
   prompt (the grey box).
3. **The next part:** open the part you just made, go to its very last frame, pause, and click **Save frame**. Make
   the next part with Frames to Video from **that saved frame**, not from the scene's picture again. That way nothing
   jumps back: the notes stay in Amit's pocket, the book stays in his hands. Paste the next prompt, and repeat for
   every part.
4. **Join them:** in the scene builder, put the parts one after another and play each join. Every part keeps moving
   to its last frame, and the next one starts talking straight away, so there is no pause between them. If a part
   still ends standing still, or its last frame catches a blink, pause a moment earlier, save that frame instead,
   trim the part to end there, and make the next part from that frame.
5. Where the place changes (into the bookstore; the box at the shoe shop's counter; the sofa, then the table at
   home), the scene has a **second shot**: make it the same way from its own picture and put it after the first.
6. Check each part: the camera stays still. The lips move only on the lines, in time with the words. Nobody's face,
   hair or clothes change. No words or subtitles appear on screen. If a part is wrong, make it again, and then the
   parts after it again too, from its new last frame.
7. Download each scene as one MP4, named as it says (for example `flow-1-home.mp4`), put them in one folder, and tell
   me where.

**Why not Extend:** Flow's Extend only works on 8-second Veo 3.1 clips (and must be done with Veo 3.1 Lite); the
10-second clips can't be extended yet. So the parts are joined through their last frames instead. If you would rather
use Extend with 8-second clips, tell me and I'll re-time the parts for that.

**The times:** each part's times count from the start of that part. They come from the game's voices, so the game's
voices fit the mouths.

**The pictures are WebP** (in `assets/story/` and `assets/party/`). If Flow won't take a WebP, open it in Preview and
choose File → Export → PNG, or ask me for PNG copies.

**Sound:** the game plays its own voices over the video: Swifty's recording, and the boy's, the father's and the
shopkeeper's voices. The video's own sound is turned off, unless you like Flow's voices better (tell me). Swifty isn't
in these videos, because the game draws her; where she explains something between two lines, the game holds the video
while she talks.
